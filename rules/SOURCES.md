# Policy and rule sources

Verified 13 September 2026. Links below are primary publishers; the project does not treat this register as legal advice.

## Highly productive land

- [NPS-HPL 2022, consolidated with December 2025 amendments](https://environment.govt.nz/publications/national-policy-statement-for-highly-productive-land-2/) — amendments took effect on 15 January 2026. Clauses 3.5(7), 3.8 and 3.9 are material to this screen.
- Clause 3.9(2)(j)(i) creates a pathway for development, operation or decommissioning of specified infrastructure where there is a functional or operational need. Clause 3.9(3) still requires loss of productive capacity to be minimised or mitigated. Therefore S-05 is a **flag**, never an automatic exclusion.
- Until operative regional HPL maps exist, clause 3.5(7) applies a transitional definition involving rural zoning and LUC 1, 2 or 3, with listed exceptions. This tool only has the LUC component, so the flag is deliberately conservative and requires planning review.
- [MPI overview of the NPS-HPL](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/agriculture/farm-management-the-environment-and-land-use/national-policy-statement-for-highly-productive-land/) — records the 2025 consultation and policy history.
- [NPS for Renewable Electricity Generation](https://environment.govt.nz/acts-and-regulations/national-policy-statements/national-policy-statement-for-renewable-electricity-generation/) — consolidated amendments also took effect on 15 January 2026.

## Technical and data references

- [MBIE, Economics of Utility-Scale Solar in Aotearoa New Zealand](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/assets/utility-scale-solar-forecast-in-aotearoa-new-zealand-v3.pdf) — precedent for broad siting assumptions and road proximity as a distribution-network proxy. A proxy does not establish voltage, capacity, connection cost or a feasible point of connection.
- [Electricity Authority, final energy prices](https://www.ea.govt.nz/data-and-insights/datasets/wholesale/dispatch-and-pricing/final-energy-prices/) — half-hourly nodal final prices.
- [LINZ Data Service](https://www.linz.govt.nz/products-services/data/linz-data-service) — boundaries, roads, topographic powerlines, DEM and basemap services.
- [LCDB v5.0](https://lris.scinfo.org.nz/layer/104400-lcdb-v50-land-cover-database-version-50-mainland-new-zealand/) — land-cover classification.
- [LENZ mean annual solar radiation](https://lris.scinfo.org.nz/layer/48095-lenz-mean-annual-solar-radiation/) — resource ranking input.
- [Manaaki Whenua LRIS portal guide](https://www.landcareresearch.co.nz/tools-and-resources/databases/lris-portal-support-and-guides/getting-started) — download workflow and registration requirement.
