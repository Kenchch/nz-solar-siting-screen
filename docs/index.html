Measured, not illustrated

The demo above runs on twelve rectangles. This runs the same geometric rules on thousands of mapped Canterbury farmland polygons against mapped transmission lines, distribution lines and road centrelines.

Distance to a road tracks the low-voltage network, which is everywhere, and says almost nothing about distance to transmission, which is what a utility-scale connection needs.

OpenStreetMap is not LINZ: completeness varies, and a mapped land-use polygon is not a parcel. One of the top-ranked sites by road proximity turned out on aerial imagery to be a coastal sand spit. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

02

Capture rate measures timing value

It compares output-weighted nodal price with the all-hours mean price. Below 100% means a megawatt-hour generated in the solar shape earns less than the time-average spot value.

The metered ISL0661 load shape runs through the identical calculation as a control. It captures above 100% in every year, so the gap is about timing, not about PV as such.

It is not a revenue forecast: PPA terms, losses, basis risk, FTRs, curtailment and connection constraints are excluded.

03

Seasonal mismatch, not midday cannibalisation

The capture rate splits exactly into a seasonal term — is the output in the expensive months — and an intraday term — is it in the expensive hours. Almost all of the discount sits in the seasonal term, because winter dry-year prices arrive when a fixed-tilt array produces least.

The intraday term is near zero today because this market has very little solar in it. It is the term that grows more negative as PV penetration rises.

AUDITABLE BY DESIGN

Rules leave a trail

  1. S-01—04Exclude

    Area, shape, land cover and conservation intersections send records to quarantine with rule IDs.

  2. S-05Flag

    LUC 1–3 triggers planning review under the amended NPS-HPL; it does not disappear from the analysis.

  3. S-06—07Rank

    Grid distance is reported twice and kept out of the score. Only solar resource and usable area order the review queue.

  4. QAStop

    Wrong CRS fails immediately. A suspiciously high reject rate aborts publication instead of silently producing a map.
